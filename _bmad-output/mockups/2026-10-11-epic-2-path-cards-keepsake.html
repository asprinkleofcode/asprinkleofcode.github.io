What was decided

TopicDecision
LayoutOne section of three path cards replaces label-only Exploration and the separate Highlights. It must not look like a second nav bar.
Card contentOptional metric and unit, an action headline, one supporting line, and "Read the story →".
RotationPrevious and next buttons with "N of M". Nothing advances on its own. Slide one must carry the pitch alone.
Click targetStretched link over the whole card, with an intentional-purpose comment in the code.
Leadership and BeyondNon-clickable "Coming soon" cards, derived from the registry with no flag. The path pages stay reachable from the header.
FieldsOptional highlight { headline, metric?, unit? } on frontmatter; required when featured is set.
AccuracyThe Watcher figure is syncs, not users. It removed manual app-access provisioning; users are still created by hand.
NamesEvery company, product, or system name needs Sprinkle's confirmation first. Only confirmed names are ever written down.
ProcessStory 2.6 rewritten, 2.4 extended, 3.1 and 4.5 re-pointed. Shipped as PR 33, docs only.
