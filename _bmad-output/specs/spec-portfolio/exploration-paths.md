# Exploration Paths

The portfolio organizes understanding of Alisha into **three distinct paths**, not one undifferentiated "Selected Work" collection. Referenced by CAP-2, CAP-3, CAP-4, CAP-5, CAP-7. Visitor-facing labels are fixed (D-15); the section headings below are internal names. Source: `PRD.md` §5.

| Path | Label (D-15) | Kind | Demonstrates | Content target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering / Technical | **Engineering** | Professional evidence | Technical ownership; complex problem-solving; business-to-engineering translation; architecture and infrastructure judgment; ambiguity handling; decisions and tradeoffs; delivery and outcomes | Preferred 3 polished visitor-ready stories; minimum 2 when evidence is strong enough that another story would dilute quality; stretch up to 5 when they materially improve the evidence |
| Leadership / Enablement | **Leadership & Enablement** | Professional evidence | Mentorship; onboarding; knowledge sharing; team development; cross-team enablement; raising the effectiveness of others | Preferred 3 polished visitor-ready stories; minimum 2 when evidence is strong enough that another story would dilute quality |
| Beyond the Code | **Beyond the Code** | Personal exploration (not a professional evidence category) | The person behind the professional identity | Exactly two dimensions for MVP: powerlifting, and Birdhouses. No third dimension and no placeholder. No arbitrary story-count requirement; depth follows the strength of the material |

## Rules

- A story qualifies for a professional path because it is meaningful evidence of one or more target capabilities, **not** because it demonstrates an interesting technology (FR-4).
- Two strong stories are preferable to three weak stories within a professional category (PRD A-4).
- Beyond the Code reveals the person; it does **not** argue that hobbies make Alisha a better engineer, and it carries no forced hobby-to-engineering metaphor (FR-13, D-7).
- A future Beyond the Code dimension may be added only if genuine material emerges; the path must never carry a placeholder (D-18).
- Relevant Leadership & Enablement evidence includes contractor / offshore onboarding, peer development conversations, and intern mentorship (PRD §5.2).
