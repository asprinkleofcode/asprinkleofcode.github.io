# Glossary

Terms used with a specific meaning across `SPEC.md` and its companions. Inherited from `PRD.md` §1.1.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Path** | One of the three top-level ways to understand Alisha: Engineering, Leadership & Enablement, Beyond the Code. |
| **Dimension** | A single personal topic within the Beyond the Code path: powerlifting; Birdhouses. |
| **Story** | A single piece of professional evidence within the Engineering or Leadership & Enablement path. |
| **Summary** | The concise, scannable representation of a story shown before a visitor commits to reading it. |
| **Deep story** | The full story experience: context, ownership, decision, rationale, tradeoffs, collaboration, outcome, reflection (see `professional-storytelling.md`). |
| **Surface** | A place in the experience where a visitor need is met — homepage recognition, a path index, a deep story, the footer. |
| **Evidence** | A concrete demonstration of how Alisha operates, as opposed to an unsupported descriptive claim. |
| **Public-safe** | Expressible in public without exposing confidential information (see `professional-storytelling.md`; PRD §7.7, C-3). |
