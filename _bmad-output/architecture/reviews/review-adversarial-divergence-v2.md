Construct two units one level down that obey every AD to the letter yet build
incompatibly. v1 findings (R1 capability vocab, R4 composite key, R8 `cssVar` fallback,
R9 build-level `draft`) are closed and are **not** re-litigated here. New surface under
attack: **AD-18**, **AD-4(c)**, **AD-11**'s OPEN hover clause, and the re-pointed
bindings (PRD v0.4 / EXPERIENCE v0.6) — plus a full sweep for pairs v1 missed.
