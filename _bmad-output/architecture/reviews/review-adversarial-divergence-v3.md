# Reviewer Gate — Lens: adversarial divergence attack (v3, update pass)

Construct two units one level down that obey every AD to the letter yet build
incompatibly. v1/v2 findings (R1–R9, D1–D9) are closed and are **not** re-litigated
here. New surface under attack: the **AD-19 amendment** (`--header-height` clause,
added for the now-sticky header) and the **new AD-20** (`personal.pdf` static-asset
field, closing the Birdhouse care-guide Deferred row) — plus a fresh sweep for anything
else these two changes touch, including the now-stale EXPERIENCE.md wording AD-20 was
supposed to retire.

**Verdict: seven new holes (D10–D16), all inside the two things that actually changed
this run.** D10–D11 are the same root cause — AD-19's "set where the header is sized"
names *who computes* the value but not *where the custom property must live* for a
sibling (`<main>`) to read it, nor whether it's static or measured; both are silent
failure modes (nothing errors, the heading just lands under the header). D12–D13 are a
matched pair on the PDF direct link: AD-20's own descriptive prose ("opens … in a new
tab") argues for `target="_blank"`, its prohibition argues against it, and the
Conventions row's name ("Downloadable / viewable") invites a `download` attribute that
would quietly break the one requirement (viewability on static hosting) the owner
actually asked for. D14–D15 are structural: AD-20 never states whether the care guide
is the Birdhouses entry itself or a distinct `listed: false` entry (AD-2's mechanism,
written before AD-20 existed, goes unreferenced), and never states which layer
(frontmatter-driven page chrome vs. AD-8's MDX whitelist) owns rendering the PDF
affordance — either gap alone produces double-rendering or silent no-ops between a
route-page builder and a content author who each followed a different, equally valid
existing AD. D16 is the most severe finding: EXPERIENCE.md v0.8 §9.3 and its Birdhouse
Flow diagram were never updated to match PRD A-12's resolution, so they still describe
the exact "lightweight page / external link acceptable" shape AD-20 was written to
retire — and the spine's own "never overrides EXPERIENCE" precedence clause, read
literally, tells a builder who hits that contradiction to prefer the stale document over
AD-20. All seven are closable by tightening AD-19/AD-20 text and patching
EXPERIENCE.md; none requires a new AD.
