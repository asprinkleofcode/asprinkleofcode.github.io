### Spec-to-implementation reconciliation

**F-1. Rationale and Tradeoffs appear twice on both live stories, contrary to a decision Story 2.2 recorded.** (AGG, ADV, RUN)
- The 2.2 decision says rationale and tradeoffs are parts of the block, not separate sections, when a block is used (`spec-2-2…md:161`, "Block composition (decided 2026-10-11)").
- The 2.4B and 2.5 specs then required both the block props *and* separate Rationale/Tradeoffs sections (`spec-2-4b…md` Boundaries "Sections, in canonical order: … Decision, Rationale, Tradeoffs …"; `spec-2-5…md` same). The epic AC's canonical list (`epics.md:594`) allows both.
- As built: `rcm-app-access-watcher.mdx:54-55` (block props), `:71` and `:91` (sections). `azure-terraform-platform.mdx:57-58` and `:82-83` (two blocks), `:88` and `:108` (sections).
- Observed on `vite preview` (`#/engineering/azure-terraform-platform`): the `dt` labels are Decision, Rationale, Tradeoffs, Decision, Rationale, Tradeoffs, and the `h2`s include Rationale and Tradeoffs as well.
- Why no story caught it: the decision lived only in the 2.2 spec, and `epic-2-context.md` was never updated with it.
- Disposition: **fix now**. Lesson: decisions made mid-epic go into the epic context (P-2).
- **Decided by Sprinkle (2026-10-12, team discussion), option (a):**
  - Decision blocks carry only `decision`.
  - Each block's rationale and tradeoffs text moves into the Rationale and Tradeoffs sections, in the same heading-then-details format as the other sections (e.g. a `###` entry or a bullet).
  - **Condition: keep the quality of the content.** Move it, never thin it. No reasoning is dropped or shortened, and Alisha reviews the reworked sections.
  - A build rule then rejects `rationale=`/`tradeoffs=` props alongside a matching section.
  - The decision is recorded in the epic context so Epic 3 inherits it.

### Diff-scope review (cross-story boundaries)

**F-5. Only the two named stories are ever rendered by a test, so "adding a story means adding one file" has no render safety net.** (VG, ECH, ADV)
- `registry.test.ts:153` and `:177` render `rcm-app-access-watcher` and `azure-terraform-platform` by slug. No test iterates `entries`.
- MDX block props are not type-checked. `storyBodyProblems` (`plugins/contentFrontmatter.ts:130-162`) checks section names, order and headings only, not imports or block props.
- So a third story with a misspelled capability, an unknown section, a unit without a metric (each throws at render), or a missing block import passes typecheck, lint, tests and build. It then fails into the ErrorBoundary on the live site.
- Disposition: **fix now, before Epic 3 adds content**. Parametrise the live test over every `work` entry.
- **Decided by Sprinkle (2026-10-12, team discussion):**
  - One parametrised live-registry test over every `work` entry: load the body, render it, assert the Context/Decision/Outcome `h2`s, and assert the highlight metric and unit appear when a highlight is set.
  - **It lands first**, before the F-1 and F-6 content changes, so it guards those edits.

**F-6. The homepage metric and the story's OutcomeBlock metric are typed twice with no check.** (ADV, ECH)
- `rcm-app-access-watcher.mdx:12-13` vs `:112-113`, and `azure-terraform-platform.mdx:12-13` vs `:132-133`. Only the per-slug tests compare them.
- **Decided by Sprinkle (2026-10-12, team discussion):**
  - A story's Outcome metric **always** matches its homepage highlight metric.
  - Fix by design, not just by test. The metric and unit live **only** in frontmatter `highlight`.
  - `WorkStory` provides `highlight` to the body through a small React context around `<Body />`. `OutcomeBlock` renders the metric and unit from that context, in the same place and styling, next to its caveat.
  - A build rule in `storyBodyProblems` rejects literal `metric=`/`unit=` on `<OutcomeBlock>` and allows at most one `OutcomeBlock` per work story.
  - Remove the duplicate props from both `.mdx` files.
  - A story that isn't featured but has a metric adds a `highlight` without `featured` (the schema already allows it).
  - Contract amendment: this relaxes Story 2.1's "components take props only" for `OutcomeBlock` (AD-7 still holds, since it is not the registry). Record the change in the spec trail and the epic context.
  - Rejected alternatives:
    - Plugin-injected frontmatter: verbose, and literals are still possible.
    - The page rendering the metric outside the block: it separates the number from its caveat (Sally).

**F-7. The schema accepts `highlight.metric` and `unit` on personal entries, but PathCard silently drops them.** (VG, ECH, ADV — all three lenses)
